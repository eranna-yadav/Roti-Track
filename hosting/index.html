FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is Roti Track free?

Yes. Tracking calories and water, today's meal plan and reminders are free. Pro adds the full 7-day plan, meal swaps, 30-day trends, custom recipes and longer reports.

Is it only for vegetarians?

No. Choose vegetarian, eggetarian or non-vegetarian, and the plan and food list follow your choice.

Does it have food from my region?

Yes. Pick North, South, East, West or all-India, and your plan uses dishes from that region, like poha, dosa, sambar, thepla or fish curry.

Can I track pizza, noodles or momos?

Yes. Along with Indian food there are popular Western and Chinese dishes, so you can log everything you eat. Your diet plan stays Indian.

Will reminders work when my phone is locked?

Yes. On some phones (like Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo) you may need to allow the app to run in the background; the app shows you how.

How do I cancel Pro?

Cancel anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions, or from the Pro screen if you paid with Razorpay. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

Is my data safe?

Your food and water logs stay on your phone. We never see your card or UPI details, don't sell your data and show no ads. Read our Privacy Policy.
