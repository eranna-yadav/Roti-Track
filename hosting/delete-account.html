Delete your Roti Track account

You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Roti Track and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, profile, logs, subscription and referral records are deleted.

Without the app

Email rotitrack.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Roti Track account”. We delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you, except payment records that Google Play or Razorpay must keep by law. Cancel any active subscription in Google Play or from the Pro screen, since deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.
