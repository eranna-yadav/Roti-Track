Roti Track helps Indians lose weight without changing the food they love. Track roti, rice, dosa, idli, biryani, chapati, dal, sabzi, snacks, water intake, and calories in just a few taps.

Get a personal Indian diet plan for weight loss, count the calories in Indian food, drink enough water and track your steps and sleep – in English, Hindi or your own language. Free to download, with an optional Pro plan for extra features.

• Veg, egg and non-veg marks on every food
• Protein tracker to help you reach your daily protein goal
