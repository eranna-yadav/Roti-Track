FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
Roti Track is a diet planner, calorie counter and water tracker made for Indian food and Indian homes. Plan your meals, count calories and drink enough water – in English or your own language.

🍛 AN INDIAN DIET PLAN MADE FOR YOU
• A daily meal plan built from your age, height, weight, activity and goal – lose, keep or gain weight
• Vegetarian, eggetarian or non-vegetarian
• North, South, East, West or all-India meals: poha, idli, dosa, dal, roti, sabzi, rice and more
• Don't like a meal? Swap it with one tap (Pro)
• See the whole week ahead with the 7-day plan (Pro)

🔥 CALORIE & MACRO COUNTER
• 190+ foods with calories, protein, carbs and fat per serving
• Indian favourites first – idli to biryani, chai to gulab jamun – plus popular Western and Chinese dishes like pizza, burgers, pasta, fried rice, noodles and momos
• Log breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner in seconds, or log a planned meal with one tap
• Veg, egg and non-veg marks on every food
• Log exercise and see calories burned
• Add your own recipes (Pro)

💧 WATER TRACKER & REMINDERS
• A daily water goal based on your weight and activity
• Log water, nimbu pani, coconut water, chaas, chai and more
• Smart water reminders through the day, with pleasant sounds
• Reminders work even when your phone is locked

🔔 MEAL REMINDERS THAT CARE
• Gentle reminders for breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner
• Friendly voice reminders that call you by name
• Quiet when your phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb

📊 PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
• Daily streaks, BMI and weight progress
• 7-day trends for calories and water, 30-day trends with Pro
• Download a PDF summary report of your meals, exercise and weight

English, हिन्दी, ಕನ್ನಡ, తెలుగు, தமிழ், मराठी, বাংলা and ગુજરાતી.
