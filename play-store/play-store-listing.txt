FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
Roti Track is a free Indian diet plan app, calorie counter and water reminder in one. Get a personal Indian diet chart for weight loss, weight gain or healthy eating, count the calories in Indian food, and drink enough water every day – in English or your own language.

Made for Indian food and Indian homes: dal, roti, rice, sabzi, idli, dosa, poha, paratha, biryani and chai are all in the app, with calories, protein, carbs and fat for every serving.

🍛 INDIAN DIET PLAN FOR WEIGHT LOSS OR GAIN
• A personal daily meal plan from your age, height, weight, activity and goal – lose weight, maintain or gain weight
• Vegetarian, eggetarian or non-vegetarian diet plans
• North Indian, South Indian, East, West or all-India meals
• Breakfast, lunch, evening snack and dinner, with the right calories for each
• Don't like a meal? Swap it with one tap (Pro)
• Plan the whole week with the 7-day diet chart (Pro)

🔥 CALORIE COUNTER & NUTRITION TRACKER
• 190+ foods with calories and macros – Indian food first, plus popular Western and Chinese dishes like pizza, burgers, pasta, fried rice, noodles and momos
• Track calories, protein, carbs and fat for every meal
• Log a food in seconds, or log your planned meal with one tap
• Veg, egg and non-veg marks on every food
• Log exercise and walking to see calories burned
• Save your own home recipes (Pro)

💧 WATER TRACKER & DRINK WATER REMINDER
• A daily water goal based on your weight, gender and activity
• Log water, nimbu pani, coconut water, chaas, chai and more
• Smart water reminders through the day, with pleasant sounds
• Reminders work even when your phone is locked

🔔 MEAL REMINDERS & VOICE REMINDERS
• Gentle reminders for breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner
• Friendly voice reminders that call you by name, in your language
• Quiet when your phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb

⏳ INTERMITTENT FASTING TIMER
• Track your fasting window – 12:12, 14:10, 16:8 and more

📊 PROGRESS, BMI & REPORTS
• BMI, weight progress and daily streaks
• 7-day calorie and water trends, 30-day trends with Pro
• Download a PDF report of your meals, exercise and weight to share with your doctor or dietitian
