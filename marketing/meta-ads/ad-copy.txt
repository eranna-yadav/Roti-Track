TARGETING (start here, then let Meta optimise)
• Location: India
• Age: 18–55   (Meta requires 18+ for weight-loss ads)
• Gender: all (try women 25–45 as a separate ad set; usually strong for diet apps)
• Interests: Weight loss, Dieting, Healthy diet, Indian cuisine, Physical fitness, Yoga, Nutrition
• Placements: Advantage+ (automatic)
• Language ads: for Ad 4, add separate ad sets by state/language (e.g. Hindi belt, Karnataka, Telangana & AP, Tamil Nadu)

BUDGET & TESTING
• Start: ₹300–500 per day for 7 days, all 4 ads in one ad set
• After 7 days: turn off the ad with the highest cost per install, put more budget on the best one
• Good result for a diet app in India: ₹8–25 per install

META HEALTH AD RULES (to avoid rejection)
• No before/after photos, no body-shaming ("Tired of being fat?")
• Don't say "you" + a body condition ("Are you overweight?")
• No promises like "lose 10 kg in 7 days"
• Target 18+ only
